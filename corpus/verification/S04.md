## Flag 1 — MISSING: conditions (subgroup sign flip under High TT)

- **Field:** conditions (omission; key_finding wording affected too)
- **Current:** "technology turbulence (negative moderator on ADM->BRS -0.23* and OPE->BRS -0.32***); leadership support (positive moderator on BRS->FP 0.29*); main effect ACRM->FP 0.53*** unconditional" — reads TT as a dampener only; key_finding says "gains shrink under technology turbulence".
- **Proposed addition:** note that in the High-TT subgroup the ADM→BRS and OPE→BRS slopes turn negative (sign flip), positive only under Low TT — i.e. under high technology turbulence the AI-side paths to relationship satisfaction reverse, they do not merely shrink. E.g. append: "…; under High TT the ADM→BRS and OPE→BRS slopes turn negative (Fig. 4)". effect_direction stays `positive` — the ACRM→FP main effect (0.53***) is unmoderated.
- **Evidence:** "The graphs show that, with the increase of ADM (for H1) and with the increase of OPE (for H2), BRS decreases as it is affected by High TT. Both graphs indicate that BRS increases in Low TT, as the gradients of the dotted lines are positive, but the gradients of the continuous lines are negative." — p. 444 (PDF p. 8, Fig. 4 discussion).
