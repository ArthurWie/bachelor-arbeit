What was checked:

- **sample:** p. 249 confirms the chain exactly — "In total, 1953 managers were approached … 1675 managers were screened out in this process, and 278 managers were qualified for the final steps … the authors finally considered 257 responses for data analysis." Time-lagged robustness subsample: "we were able to obtain data from a subsample of 93 participants three months after study 4 (response rate 31.3%)" (p. 250). Qualtrics via research-firm database, random sampling (p. 249). All matches.
- **country_region / industry:** Bangladesh RMG export manufacturers — "the study considered a country—Bangladesh, which heavily relies on RMG manufacturing and export" (p. 243). Author affiliations (Australia/USA/UK/Bangladesh) correctly not conflated with the sample.
- **outcome_construct / ca_measure:** SCA scale (Table 2, p. 248): "how well has your firm performed its _____ goals relative to competitors in the last three years? SCA1: Market share growth, SCA2: Sales growth, SCA3: Profitability, SCA4: Return on investment." The row's boundary-case annotation (relative-performance-vs-competitors coded as CA with a flag) is exactly the case-law rule this study itself established — consistent.
- **effect_direction = conditional:** the structural model (Fig. 4, p. 250) contains no direct MAC→SCA path and no direct AI→SCA path; AI enters only as moderator of MAC→sensing/seizing/reconfiguring (H4a–c). Table 3 (p. 249) shows the AAI–SCA correlation at 0.029 — no standalone AI–outcome association. Under case law rule 2 (effect exists only via mechanism/condition), `conditional` is correct.
- **conditions:** moderator coefficients verified p. 249 — "AI adoption moderates the relationship of MAC-MSN (β = 0.104, p < 0.05), MAC-MSI (β = 0.131, p < 0.05), and MAC-MRN (β = 0.128, p < 0.05)." Mediation verified p. 249 — "MAC-MSN-SCA (β = 0.243, p < 0.001), MAC-MSI-SCA (β = 0.160, p < 0.01), and MAC-MRN-SCA (β = 0.150, p < 0.01)." The model specifies mediation-only (no direct MAC→SCA path), so "carry MAC->SCA fully" is a fair description. Control variables (firm size β = −0.001 n.s., analytics experience β = 0.005 n.s., respondent position β = 0.075 n.s., p. 250) are robustness controls, not conditions under rule 4 — correctly omitted.
- **method = survey-SEM:** interviews (studies 1b, 2b) and Delphi/expert phases (study 3b) served problem verification and instrument development only; all AI→outcome evidence comes from the PLS-SEM survey (studies 4–5). Correct under case law rule 3; already noted in quality_notes.
- **ai_measure:** AAI 4-item adoption scale (Table 2, p. 248), adapted from Rahman, Hossain & Fattah (2021). Matches.
- **quality_notes:** objective-subjective correlation r = 0.67 confirmed p. 250 ("generates a strong positive correlation (r = 0.67)").

Minor observations, none flag-worthy under case law: (a) the paper's theoretical base is RBV *plus* dynamic capabilities (p. 242) while the row records only dynamic capabilities — DC is the operative lens (sensing/seizing/reconfiguring are the modeled constructs), RBV is framing; (b) the paper quantifies the moderation in R² terms (sensing 46.9%→48.2%, seizing 48.5%→50.5%, reconfiguring 51.6%→53.5% with AI adoption, p. 250) — additional detail, not a threshold requiring coding.
