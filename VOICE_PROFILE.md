# Voice Profile — Arthur Wienerroither

Derived 2 July 2026 from: 3 English school essays (2021, `voice_samples/*.docx`), German VWA
(29 pp., bodybuilding nutrition, `voice_samples/VWA.docx.pdf`), 3 German Textsorten exercises
(`voice_samples/school_texts_german.md`), anchored against the approved seminar-paper register
(`Seminar Arbeit/sections/*.tex` — the calibrated target; where samples and anchor conflict,
the anchor wins).

Use during drafting and in the rewrite pass alongside `rewrite_standards.md`. The goal is
prose that reads like Arthur on a good day — not like his school essays, and not like an
AI's idea of perfect English.

## Signature traits — EMULATE these

1. **Claim-first paragraphs, one aspect each.** He argues dimension by dimension (convenience
   → safety → spending control; misconception → habits → recommendation), each paragraph
   opening with its claim. Evidence: cash essay, clothing report, seminar discussion.
2. **Concession → rebuttal.** States the opposing view fairly, then knocks it down directly:
   "One of the reasons why people still hold onto… but they are mistaken." Use for handling
   contradictory studies. Evidence: cash essay, Meinungsrede ("Ja, wir haben… Wieso muss man
   deswegen…").
3. **Colon before elaboration.** He introduces specifics, lists, and verdicts with a colon —
   in both languages ("Ganz ehrlich: …"; "Both agree on one practical point: …"). Keep this;
   it is his most consistent punctuation habit.
4. **Numbers pulled forward.** He leads with quantities when he has them (80 percent, 40 years,
   1.77%). Perfect fit for an SLR — let counts and effect sizes carry arguments.
5. **Short verdict sentence to close a point.** After a long evidence sentence, a flat
   conclusion: "It is just not possible." / "This thesis addresses that gap." Also satisfies
   rewrite_standards §6.1 rhythm.
6. **Mid-sentence connectives.** however/jedoch/therefore placed inside the sentence, not as
   openers — his natural habit, already codified in rewrite_standards §2.
7. **Necessity modals when recommending.** He writes "zwingend", "unumgänglich", "imperative",
   "must" — recommendations sound committed, not "could perhaps consider".
8. **Direct stance-taking.** He is willing to call a position wrong and say what follows.
   In the thesis: state clearly which claims the evidence supports and which it contradicts.
9. **Stepwise method narration.** "Um festzustellen, welche Diät…, ist es notwendig, sich
   alle Arten… genauer anzuschauen" — announce the analytical step, then take it. Use in
   the Method chapter.
10. **Concrete grounding.** Abstract claims get an everyday or real-world anchor. In the
    thesis: name real firms/settings from the reviewed studies rather than staying abstract.

## Natural tendencies — TEMPER for academic register

- **Rhetorical questions** (both languages, incl. closing questions to the reader) → convert
  to statements; none in the thesis.
- **Absolutes and overstatement** ("100% safer", "99 Prozent falsch", "millions of ways",
  "ten times more") → replace with the actual number or a single calibrated hedge.
- **Reader address and appeals** ("Redet miteinander!", "you") → none.
- **Enthusiasm intensifiers** ("unbelievably", "unglaublich", "horrendous") → drop; let
  evidence carry weight (rewrite_standards §7.1).

## Non-native texture — how to keep it authentic

- Prefer plain, common constructions over polished rare idioms. He uses simple idioms
  naturally ("beat by a landslide") and calques German ones ("put his hand into fire") —
  do NOT inject literary/native-rare idioms he would never produce.
- Do NOT replicate his mechanical errors (comma before that-clauses, "criteria's") — those
  get fixed by Grammarly anyway; errors are not voice.
- Vocabulary level: B2/C1 with occasional formal flourish ("I feel obligated", "imperative").
  If a word feels like it belongs in The Economist, pick the plainer synonym.

## Priority order when rules conflict

1. `WRITING_STANDARDS.md` (citation, register, structure)
2. `rewrite_standards.md` (banned patterns)
3. This file (voice)

A voice trait never justifies breaking a standard; within the standards, prefer his patterns.
